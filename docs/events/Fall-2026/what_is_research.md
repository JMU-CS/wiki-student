Hello CS/IT Family,

Please join us for our annual "What is Computer Science and Information Technology Research?" talk hosted by the JMU CS/IT Research Seminar this Friday (9/11) from 11:20-12:30 in King 136. Come hear CS & IT faculty members discuss primary research in computer science & information technology as well as give short flash talks on a variety of CS & IT research areas.

## Program

1. 15-Minute Overview: "What is CS/IT Research?" by Profs. Lam and Weikle
2. 2- to 3-Minute Flash Talks by a variety of faculty members who work in different CS & IT research areas.

## Details

- Friday, Sept. 11
- 11:20 am to 12:30 pm
- 136 King Hall

## Abstract

Join us for an overview of computer science and information technology research followed by 2- to 3-minute flash talks giving you an overview of ongoing research within the JMU Computer Science department. Whether you are looking to get into research or just interested in learning more, you are welcome!
