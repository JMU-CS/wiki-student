Join **Meredythe (Fallon) Del Gallo(‘12)**, **Jodi Fox (‘26M)**, JMU alumnae, alongside current JMU student entrepreneurs and [Fallon Challenge](https://www.thefallonchallenge.com/) finalists for a conversation about ideas, entrepreneurship, and what it takes to start building.

## Two chances!

1. Tue 9/15/2026 4:00 PM - 5:00 PM in Hartman 1053
2. Wed 9/16/2026 12:30 PM - 1:30 PM in Hartman 1053

Hear from students who are doing it right now. Learn how they got started, what they’re building, what they’ve learned, and where they hope to go next. Connect with an alumna whose JMU entrepreneurship legacy continues to create opportunities for the next generation.

!!! info "Not a Lecture"
    ![mortarboard with caption in cursive "this is not a class" rendered as if a painting parodying in Magritte's Treachery of Images](./not_a_class.png)
    This isn't a lecture or a classroom workshop. **It's an invitation into JMU's entrepreneurship community.**

!!! tip "You're invited!"
    You don't need a business. You don't need an idea. You just need to be curious.
    Come meet the people. Hear their stories. Make connections. [Find your place in the community](https://www.jmu.edu/cob/centers/center-for-entrepreneurship/index.shtml).
